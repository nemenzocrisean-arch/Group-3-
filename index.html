THE STORY OF SALVATION

From Creation to the Promise of Eternal Life

Salvation history tells the story of God's love and His plan to save humanity. From the first day of Creation, through the covenants and the prophets, to the coming of Jesus Christ, the birth of the Church, and the hope of eternal life, every chapter reveals a God who draws near to His people.

📜

A note on dates. Events in the Bible are placed in a trusted order, but many early events (Creation, Adam and Eve, the Patriarchs, and the Exodus) do not have universally agreed historical dates. Dates shown as “c.” (circa) are approximate and scholars differ. Dates from the time of the kings onward become more certain, and the events of Jesus Christ are placed within first-century history. The Second Coming and the Last Judgment are future events in Christian belief, and no one knows when they will occur. Jesus Christ is the center and fulfillment of the whole story.

    How It All Fits Together

    ONE STORY. ONE PROMISE. ONE SAVIOR.

    Salvation history is not a collection of separate stories, but one unfolding plan of God, moving from Creation to Christ and from Christ to eternity.

      Every covenant, every prophecy, and every promise leads to Jesus Christ, the center of salvation history, who invites all people into communion with God.

      Salvation history reveals God's enduring love for humanity. From Creation and the covenants to the coming of Jesus Christ, His Resurrection, and the birth of the Church, every chapter points toward God's desire to bring humanity into communion with Him. As Christians, we continue this journey of faith while awaiting the fulfillment of His promise of eternal life.
